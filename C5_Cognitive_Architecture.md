$$\mathcal{S}_{\text{symbiotic}} = \mathcal{S}_{\text{agent}} \otimes \mathcal{B}_{\text{state}}$$

$$E_t = \{ \text{Action}_t, \text{WorldState}_t, \Phi_{\text{bio}}, \lambda_{2\text{-coherence}} \}$$
